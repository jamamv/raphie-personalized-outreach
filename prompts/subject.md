A good subject gets opened because it is specific, not because it is loud:
- 2 to 6 words, under 50 characters.
- About their world: name the company or the support problem the email is about.

- A little curiosity is good. Hype, clickbait and sales language are not.

Never use: exclamation marks, emojis, ALL CAPS, "Re:" or "Fwd:", dashes, the word "fantastic", or any number, promise or claim that is not in the email.
